About Pixel Idle RPG

Pixel Idle RPG is a specialized incremental clicker game where players can experience the growth of a legendary miner. This web-based simulation focuses on strategic upgrades and efficient resource management.

How to Play

  • Manual Mining: Tap the main button to gather gold manually.
  • Equipment Upgrades: Spend your gold to increase Attack Power. Higher ATK means more gold per click and faster automatic production.
  • Revenue Boost: Use the 'Ad Buff' feature to double your mining efficiency for a limited time.

Technical Roadmap

Currently in Version 1.0. We are planning to implement a 'Pet System' for automated clicking and a 'Rebirth System' for long-term progression. Our goal is to provide a seamless idle gaming experience on both mobile and desktop platforms.
